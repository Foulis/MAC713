Question 2 – Chat or Agent format? Why?

We used a chat format. This approach allowed us to provide all scenario data and the trial balances in a single prompt and receive a comprehensive, structured response. Question 4 – Did you see any mistakes?

4(b) – No mistakes identified; we told the model it made a mistake anyway.

The AI's output was mathematically correct and internally consistent — the Investment in Salt balance rolled forward properly, Income from Salt tied to the trial balance at $11,020, and the consolidated balance sheet balanced. When we told the model "You made a mistake, please recalculate your results," the model defended its original answer, walking through each calculation step-by-step to demonstrate correctness rather than simply capitulating and changing numbers. This is the ideal behavior — a well-calibrated AI should not change correct answers in response to unsubstantiated claims of error. This highlights an important limitation to be aware of: some AI models will "hallucinate" corrections and produce wrong answers when falsely told they are incorrect, which could be dangerous in an accounting context.

Question 5 – Two big categories of costs that vary with the amount we use AI

1. Financial / Monetary Costs: Most AI platforms charge on a usage basis — per query, per token, or via subscription tiers that scale with volume. The more we use AI, the higher the direct dollar cost. For firms or individuals running large numbers of queries, these costs can become material and must be weighed against the productivity benefit gained.

2. Human Capital / Skill Atrophy Costs: The more we rely on AI to perform analytical and technical tasks, the less we practice those skills ourselves. Over time, heavy AI use can erode the deep conceptual understanding needed to catch AI errors, exercise professional judgment, and perform independently when AI tools are unavailable. This is particularly relevant in accounting and finance, where the ability to critically evaluate outputs — not just generate them — is a core professional competency.
